Why Choose SecureVote?

🛡️

Duplicate Prevention

Unique database-level constraints guarantee that once a voter submits a ballot, their registration token for that election is spent. Double voting is physically impossible.

⚡

Instant Tallying

Results are calculated in real-time as soon as votes are cast. See live statistics, charts, and vote shares formatted automatically through clean graphical visualizations.

🔑

Identity Validation

Built on top of Django's industry-standard authentication system. Voter login credentials and passwords are encrypted using strong salted PBKDF2 hashing algorithms.
